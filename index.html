About Me

Combining consumer psychology with modern data workflows

In the app economy, users make a download decision within 3 to 6 seconds. This realization shifted my focus from pure academic psychology to Consumer Behavior, Growth Marketing, and Storefront Optimization.

I specialize in understanding the cognitive mechanics of choice—such as choice overload, visual friction, and decision fatigue—and transforming them into measurable growth. I design end-to-end A/B tests on creative store assets, query and analyze behavioral data, and model user flows. With a background in psychology and an analytical toolkit, I help product and marketing teams build storefronts that reduce friction and maximize conversion rates.
